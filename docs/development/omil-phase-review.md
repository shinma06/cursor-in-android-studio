# Event-driven phase review

Apply only when OMIL/PM work is actually assigned. These event-driven checks supplement the [prior consultation](github-workflow.md#operations-profile). Agreed delivery and immediate preservation continue while a new decision awaits OMIL. Review purpose, guarantees, methods, assumptions, roles and burden when material evidence changes, waiting/rework repeats without new information, an intermediate outcome is reached, or the assigned phase ends/freezes. Preserve the real human start/pause and quality boundaries.

## Stop and method decisions

When an agent-selected prerequisite, schedule or evidence-success condition prevents its intended observation, or a proposed stop expands to independent obligations, connect the missing fact to one finite OMIL/PM decision before repeating the procedure. Trace the constraint to its actual source. Preserve human pauses, safety/permission/resource boundaries and required guarantees; a chosen method is not the guarantee. An unattempted route stays unattempted and an unexplained failure stays FAIL/unknown.

PM records a concrete reason to retain the method, or prospectively changes the actual assignment, prerequisite/dependency and residual owner within existing authority. A policy edit or advice receipt alone does not change that allocation. Continue independent authorized work and an already-authorized equivalent observation route with unchanged ownership/guards/bounds; OMIL response or GO is not a new dependency. This is not mandatory method churn, exhaustive search or a retry-count gate.

Safety STOP and owned return precede product evidence assessment. After actual owned writers/processes stop and the existing restoration/preservation, custody and factual return conditions are satisfied, return the shared lease without waiting for product PASS, offline sealing or OMIL response. Retain the original failure/unknown and name the remaining assessment/packaging owner. A stop message alone or an indeterminate write/ownership does not prove safe return; hold only the genuinely affected resource under its existing owner until resolved. Use the existing [stop flow](omil-portable.md#reconsider-before-stopping-unfinished-work) and [GUI return contract](gui-coordination.md), without another framework.

Reuse existing Issue/PR/plan, original observations and reports. Select a finite relevant sample including ordinary success, exceptions/incomplete work and assisted changes; expand only when a concrete unresolved fact can change the decision. Do not copy ARB phase numbers, sample/time limits, readiness results or measurements into this project's acceptance. Actual bounds and reason belong in the assignment. Code review and operational-substance assessment have distinct purposes.

Record only missing material facts: fixed period/revision/purpose, guarantee and source, who noticed the issue and human assistance, alternative/reason/authority, actual result/side effects, unknowns and residual owner. Do not require private reasoning transcripts, new reports for every operation or a parallel ledger. Include cancelled/deferred/failed work; usage, elapsed time, human burden and product quality are different measurements. Missing values remain unmeasured.

Verify an actual authorized route and receiver. Use the same event identity/reference for equivalent redelivery; inspect send outcomes before resending. Reuse completed results rather than ACK/review loops. Absent/stopped/archived recipients do not authorize restart, new roles, human forwarding as the normal route or polling/jobs. Keep unresolved custody in the existing record and end the event after returning its result.
